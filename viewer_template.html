The policy never sees the picture. It sees the 12 corner numbers, plus the drone's position, velocity, attitude and last command. In this simulator those numbers are exact. On the real Swift drone, a neural network finds the corners in greyscale camera frames, and a Kalman filter combines them with visual-inertial odometry, so the policy gets noisier, drifting versions of the same numbers.
